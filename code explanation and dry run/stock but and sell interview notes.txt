📘 Stock Buy & Sell – Deep Interview Notes
1. Question

Given an array where:

prices[i]

represents the stock price on the ith day.

Find the maximum profit by:

Buying once
Selling once
Selling after buying

If no profit is possible, return 0.

Example

prices = [7,1,5,3,6,4]

Answer = 5
2. Observation ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Question ko "Buy & Sell" mat samjho.

Question ko is tarah socho:

Har din imagine karo ki aaj stock bechna hai.

Bas ek hi question poochna hai:

"Agar aaj bechun, to maine ab tak sabse sasta stock kis price par kharida hota?"

Isi observation se O(n) solution nikalta hai.

12. Interview Explanation

"Instead of checking every buy-sell pair, I maintain the minimum stock price seen so far. For every day's price, I calculate the profit if I sell today using that minimum price. If this profit is greater than the current maximum, I update it. This allows me to find the maximum profit in a single traversal with O(n) time and O(1) extra space."

13. Common Mistakes
❌ Mistake 1

Profit pehle calculate karna.

profit = price - min_price

if price < min_price:
    min_price = price

Ye logic confusing ho sakta hai.

Interview me pehle minimum update karo.

Fir profit calculate karo.

❌ Mistake 2

Maximum price dhoondhna.

Bahut log sochte hain

Maximum Price - Minimum Price

Ye galat hai.

Kyun?

Minimum future me bhi aa sakta hai.

Impossible.

❌ Mistake 3

14. One-Line Revision

"Keep track of the cheapest stock seen so far. For every new price, assume you sell today and calculate the profit. Update the maximum profit if it's better."

⭐ Interview Shortcut (30-second Revision)
Observation: Buy before sell, maximize profit.
Variables: min_price, max_profit.
Logic: Track the minimum price seen so far. For each day's price, compute price - min_price and update the maximum profit.
Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(1)
Pattern: Running Minimum + Greedy.
